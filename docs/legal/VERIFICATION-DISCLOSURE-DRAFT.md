# Verification and disclosure — IronFleet harness

> **DRAFT — PENDING R-1.** Working draft aligned to the courts' *Guidelines for
> use of GenAI in Courts and Tribunals: Non-lawyers* (7 Dec 2023) and DECISIONS
> R-1 Q6. Not for publication until ruled. Decision points marked **[DP]**.

## 1. Your verification duty (stated to every user)

- **You are responsible for everything you file.** The harness generates drafts
  and research aids; it does not decide your case and its output is not legal
  advice.
- **Check every citation, quote and fact before use.** Generative AI can invent
  cases, quotations and provisions that look real but do not exist. The harness
  helps: it grounds drafts in your matter's own stored documents and marks which
  quotes it could verify against source text — but a verification mark is an aid,
  not a guarantee, and unverifiable material is flagged for you to resolve.
- **This tool is not a sole or main source of legal information.** If you can,
  check with a lawyer — especially before filing anything.
- **Enter nothing confidential** — nothing private, privileged, suppressed or not
  in the public domain, where the forum's rules forbid it. If such material has
  been entered, contact the registry as the courts' guidance directs.

## 2. Disclosure of AI use (current rule, stated at filing time)

The courts' guidelines (7 Dec 2023, non-lawyers) currently require disclosing
GenAI use **only if the court or tribunal asks** — there is no standing duty to
disclose. The harness states this rule wherever filing guidance is shown, and
will surface any registry requirement it is told about. **[DP — R-1 Q6: confirm
the rule holds for outputs of a paid tool, and decide whether the product should
disclose anyway for defensibility.]**

## 3. What the product claims — and never claims

The product's claim is **maximum defensibility**, never "courts will accept this
output". It never claims its documents meet filing requirements on their own, is
never presented as lawyer-checked unless a lawyer checked it (Release B's
supervised pilot is that lane), and its accuracy claims are the measured,
published numbers (data/eval reports), not assurances. **[DP — R-1 Q8: confirm
this claim set under s 23 LCA / FTA s 45.]**

## 4. Known limitations stated to users

NZ training-data limits (the courts' guidance says chatbots' NZ law knowledge can
be limited or out of date); hallucinated citations and quotes even after
verification aids; bias risks including for Māori and Pasifika context (courts'
guidance duty 4); the moot-gate and flaw ledger reduce but do not eliminate weak
arguments reaching a draft. The guidelines are under revision — **re-verify the
current courts' guidance at use time** (RNZ, July 2026 report).
