## Costed hazards carried into Path A (Module E / A-2 inputs)

1. **`experimental.text.complete`** — the model-output annotation seam rides an *experimental*
   hook in 1.18.25. Priced at M0: pin the version; re-verify the hook on every upgrade.
2. **Tool-result envelopes** — blanket text annotation corrupts built-in tools' structured
   envelopes (SchemaError, session killed — observed live). Module E annotates the
   human-facing presentation layer / custom tools' outputs only; detection + blocking already work.
3. **Operational seams** (live-observed, Windows): CLI newline truncation, orphaned opencode.exe
   needing tree-kill (`taskkill /T /F`) supervision, instance steering via PWD/git, Bun >64 KB
   stdin deadlock (file transport), concurrent-hook serialization, and a **~8× prompt-token
   scaffolding tax** per legal query (candidate cost datum for A-7).

Module E repricing input: 9 seams; observed effort went to the operational hazards (~6 mount
iterations per candidate), not the two ports themselves.
